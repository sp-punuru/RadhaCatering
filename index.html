Why Choose Us

The Comfort of Pure Homestyle Cooking

100% Pure Vegetarian

Party Planning Guide

Which Tray Size Do You Need?

We make portion planning easy for your birthday parties, poojas, and get-togethers.

Half Tray (Approx. 9" x 13")

Ideal for 10 to 12 guests as part of a multi-dish menu, or up to 15 guests for lighter appetites. Perfect for family dinners and small gatherings.

Full Tray (Approx. 12" x 20")

Ideal for 20 to 25 guests. Generous portions packed with flavor, ideal for larger celebrations, pooja feasts, and celebrations.
